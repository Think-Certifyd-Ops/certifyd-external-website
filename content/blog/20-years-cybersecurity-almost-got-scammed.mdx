## Experience does not verify a request

A familiar business pattern can make a fraudulent request plausible. Review the requested action through an established independent contact route, especially when it involves account access, payment or an authentication code.

The [NCSC phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) recommends layered technical and organisational defences. Staff need a way to ask questions and report suspicious activity; training does not make every attack detectable.

## Keep the conversation separate from product proof

The related [Gone Phishing episode](/podcast/20-years-cybersecurity-almost-got-scammed/) remains available. This article does not repeat a guest quotation or detailed case claim as independent verification of events.
