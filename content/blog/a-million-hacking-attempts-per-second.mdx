This article is part of Certifyd’s historical security editorial archive. It was revised in October 2026 to remove unsupported claims and earlier product promises. It is not a specification of currently available Certifyd features.


## Treat a headline number as a claim to examine

An attack-rate claim needs its measurement period, system boundary and definition of an attempt. This article does not independently substantiate a million attacks per second or use that number to estimate another organisation's risk.

The related [Gone Phishing episode](/podcast/a-million-hacking-attempts-per-second/) remains available as a recorded conversation. Guest experiences and observations are distinct from a published measurement dataset.

## Use a layered response

The [NCSC phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) combines technical controls, reporting and incident response. Message metadata, familiar voices and visual inspection can all have limits; no single cue authenticates every request.

This historical article does not claim that a particular open-source model supplies voice cloning or that Certifyd authenticates every interaction. Its current [worker-records offer](/platform/records/) concerns workforce evidence, review dates and attendance.
