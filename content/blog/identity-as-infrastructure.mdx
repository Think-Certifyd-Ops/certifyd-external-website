## Define what is shared

An identity service can share evidence or a claim within a defined arrangement. The receiving organisation still needs to understand the source, date, permitted purpose and requirements for its decision.

Infrastructure language can hide those boundaries. A persistent profile does not prove that every future interaction involves the same person or that all evidence remains appropriate for every role.

## Keep legal checks separate

The [Home Office employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) defines prescribed right-to-work processes. A profile is not a universally transferable statutory excuse. Data sharing also needs appropriate purpose and access controls.

Certifyd's current [records workflow](/platform/records/) helps workforce teams organise evidence, review dates and attendance. Earlier claims about eliminating categories of fraud or authenticating every business interaction are historical ideas, not available product guarantees.
