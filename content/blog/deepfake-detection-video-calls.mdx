## Read the evaluation before the percentage

[Published PNAS research](https://perception.jhu.edu/files/PDFs/22_Deepfakes/GrohEtAl_DeepfakeDetection_2022_PNAS.pdf) examines human and machine performance on defined deepfake tasks. A benchmark result is not automatically performance on every live business call.

This article does not substantiate its earlier live-call detection percentage or invented incident narrative. Results depend on the material, participants, task and evaluation conditions.

## Use controlled decisions

An organisation should not approve a payment or disclose a secret solely because a video appears genuine. Use established independent approval and contact routes and retain appropriate evidence of the decision.
