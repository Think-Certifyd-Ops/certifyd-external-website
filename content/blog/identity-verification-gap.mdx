## Define the question being answered

A record may establish that evidence was received or reviewed. It may not establish that a later caller is the same person, that a payment is authorised or that the evidence covers a different job.

For suspicious communications, the [NCSC guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) recommends layered controls and incident response. One QR code or profile cannot resolve every channel's authentication needs.

## Keep estimates labelled

Forecasts of global cybercrime cost and dated breach-survey percentages are not observed totals for current UK identity fraud. This archive does not repeat the earlier unsupported numbers or use them to calculate business risk.

Certifyd's current [workforce records](/platform/records/) support evidence organisation and human review. They do not authenticate every phone, email, meeting and doorstep interaction.
