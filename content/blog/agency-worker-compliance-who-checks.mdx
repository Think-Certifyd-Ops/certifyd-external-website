Start with the actual working arrangement. Record who engages the worker, who supplies them and where they will work.

## Identify who is responsible for the check

The [Home Office employer guide, updated on 1 October 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide), addresses agency arrangements and extended liability. For a standard arrangement where an employment business directly engages workers and supplies them into a hirer’s own operations, the guide says that the employment business remains responsible for the right-to-work check. It does not intend routine duplication by the hirer.

The wider rules depend on the contractual arrangement and how it operates. Review the current guidance for your own model, particularly when a chain supplies work or services onwards. Do not assume that every party automatically receives a penalty for the same worker.

## Agree what the placement needs

Prepare a placement record with:

- the worker and the role;
- the client, workplace and planned assignment;
- the evidence required for that work;
- the organisation responsible for each check;
- the reviewer and the location of the check result;
- the next review date and action, where applicable.

Agree these details before asking the worker to send documents. This gives the agency and client a shared starting point for their review.

## Distinguish evidence from the completed review

A worker’s uploaded passport or qualification is supplied evidence. Record the completed check or review separately: the method, reviewer, date, evidence inspected and outcome.

The worker may hold personal credentials in their app while the agency maintains its own requirements, placement information and review history. Share the information the client needs through the agreed permissions and process.

## Connect the arriving person to the placement

Follow the person, assignment and recorded arrival. If someone different arrives, identify the substitution and resolve the information your team needs before making its decision.

An arrival record is useful context. It does not complete a prescribed right-to-work check or establish that every placement requirement has been met.

## Prepare for the next assignment

Compare the worker’s evidence with the next client’s requirements. Review the relevant dates, request missing evidence and name the next reviewer.
