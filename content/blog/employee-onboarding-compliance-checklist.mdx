An onboarding checklist should tell the manager whether the person is authorised for the actual work and what remains unresolved. A collection of uploads cannot answer that question without a review.

## Define the person, role and arrangement

Record the employer or supplying organisation, proposed role, work location and planned start. Identify the party responsible for each check. Use the [current employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) for the relevant contractual arrangement rather than assuming every supplied worker requires duplicated checks by agency and hirer.

Complete the prescribed initial right-to-work process at the required time before work begins. Inspect restrictions against the actual job and retain the result and check date. A missing check can affect the employer's statutory excuse, but it does not by itself prove the person has no permission to work.

## Review role-specific requirements

Identify qualifications, professional registration, safeguarding and safety requirements. Use the relevant sector rules. DBS check eligibility depends on the role, and certificates have no official expiry date. [KCSIE paragraph 326](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6ac3c09c11ed58342ad8e4ac/Keeping_children_safe_in_education_2026.pdf) provides a bounded example where a school or college may allow work before an enhanced certificate arrives, with supervision and all other checks completed, including a separate barred-list check. It excludes starting before the certificate arrives in settings covered by EYFS. Do not apply that exception across other sectors.

Keep permission and suitability decisions separate. A person may meet one requirement while another needs review. Restrict sensitive evidence to appropriate reviewers and give the scheduler the decision and any limits they need.

## Record the start decision and next action

List each outstanding item, owner and deadline. The authorised manager should record the deployment decision and its basis. A “complete” flag should reflect reviewed requirements, rather than documents merely being received.

Set subsequent review dates from the evidence and applicable rules, including [follow-up checks](/blog/right-to-work-follow-up-checks/). Do not turn every visa or document date into an automatic termination instruction.
