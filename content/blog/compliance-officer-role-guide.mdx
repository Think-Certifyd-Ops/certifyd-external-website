A compliance owner needs to know which action comes next, who can take it and what evidence will close it. A national enforcement headline cannot prioritise an organisation's actual worker-record queue.

## Map the responsibilities

List the frameworks relevant to the workforce: right to work, sponsorship, safeguarding, professional registration, employment practice and data protection. Identify the authorised owner for each decision. They do not all have the same regulator or reporting process.

Use the [Home Office employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) for prescribed checks and [sponsor Part 3](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-3-sponsor-duties-and-compliance/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-3-sponsor-duties-and-compliance-accessible) for sponsor duties. Worker and organisational reporting events have different deadlines; a universal ten-day rule is inaccurate.

## Create an actionable queue

Distinguish a missing source result, a decision needed and an upcoming review. Attach the relevant person, role, owner and due date. Record why a deadline applies rather than generating one from every document's printed expiry date.

A physical biometric residence permit is not a substitute for the required online result. This does not mean every historic compliant check must be repeated simply because the document format changed. Review the applicable follow-up rules and actual circumstances.

## Close actions with evidence

Keep the completed result, reviewer, date and decision. Reconcile sponsor reports against actual submissions, not drafts. If an event was missed, record the facts and seek advice on the corrective action instead of silently moving its date.

Escalate uncertainty about work permission or adverse employment decisions to the appropriate specialist. A missing check can affect the employer's excuse but is not, by itself, proof of illegal work or a payable penalty.
