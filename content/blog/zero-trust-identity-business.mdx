## Define the access decision

Zero-trust principles concern explicitly assessed access within a system. They do not mean one identity check proves every future action is safe or authorised.

The [NCSC guidance on phishing](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) demonstrates why prevention, reporting and response need multiple controls. National fraud filings also need their period and database scope before they inform a business discussion.

## Avoid unsupported legal or commercial outcomes

This article does not substantiate earlier claims about automatic insurance benefits, liability reduction or universal meeting authentication. Those outcomes depend on actual contracts, law, system scope and evidence.

Certifyd currently supports [worker evidence and review actions](/platform/records/). Historical universal-security descriptions are not available product guarantees or a replacement for controlled access and approvals.
