Sponsor licence statistics describe administrative activity over a defined period. They do not explain why a particular organisation lost its licence or predict the outcome of your next compliance review.

## Keep the calendar year and rolling year distinct

The Home Office [sponsorship transparency data](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a8c3bc8d166fab554a3c3fe/sponsorship-transparency-data-apr-to-jun-2026.ods) records 3,100 Skilled Worker sponsor licence revocations during calendar year 2025. The year ending March 2025 is a different period, with 1,560 Skilled Worker revocations. Labelling both periods as “last year” obscures a material difference.

Use the SC_01 table and state the period alongside the number. Do not mix revocations with suspensions, refusals or the number of licensed organisations. They measure different things.

## Avoid unsupported explanations

A total does not establish how many decisions arose from a particular sector, missing check, reporting failure or fraudulent application. A claim about causes needs the corresponding published breakdown or decision evidence.

The sponsor register is a current snapshot. An organisation disappearing between snapshots may require investigation, but the comparison alone does not establish a revocation or its date. Our [sponsor register](/sponsors/) labels observed changes and their source dates rather than presenting them as licence decisions.

## Use the figures to prompt a records review

Check your organisation against [current sponsor duties](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-3-sponsor-duties-and-compliance/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-3-sponsor-duties-and-compliance-accessible), its own correspondence and [Appendix D](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/keep-records-for-sponsorship-appendix-d). Reconcile worker roles, pay, attendance, contact details and reports. Prioritise actual discrepancies over a numerical risk score derived from a national total.

If the Home Office issues a notice, its findings and deadlines govern the response. Get advice on those facts; a press headline cannot determine the position of the sponsor or its workers.
