Fast hiring and last-minute shift changes make it easy for a manager to inherit an unclear record. Build the handoff around the actual person and work before the shift begins.

## Complete the applicable initial process

Use the [current employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) to choose and conduct the eligible check at the required time. Retain the employer result, check date and any restrictions. A worker's own status screenshot or a physical biometric residence permit is not a substitute for the required online result.

For a Student visa holder, review the applicable work limits and obtain relevant term information through the required process. Do not infer full-time permission from a CV or a statement that the person is available.

## Give the scheduler a usable decision

The scheduler needs to know which duties and hours are authorised, whether a requirement is unresolved and who can review it. They do not necessarily need broad access to immigration documents.

For supplied workers, clarify the agency's statutory check and the receiving organisation's placement evidence. A general rule requiring every hirer to duplicate every agency check is not supported. See [agency-worker checks](/blog/agency-worker-compliance-who-checks/).

## Review changes and gaps

When the booked worker changes, reconcile the substitute with the placement requirements. Set follow-up dates under the applicable rules, rather than assuming every immigration status needs an annual repeat check.

The civil penalty maxima are [£45,000 for a first breach and £60,000 for a repeat finding within the relevant period](/blog/right-to-work-penalties-2026/). These are not automatic charges for missing paperwork. Establish the person's permission and the employer's position before drawing a conclusion.
