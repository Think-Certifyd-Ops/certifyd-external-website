A Graduate visa can allow a person to work without your organisation sponsoring that employment. Before they start, confirm their individual permission through the appropriate right-to-work check and read any restrictions shown.

Do not infer their work permission, expiry date or need for sponsorship from the fact that they recently graduated.

## Check the duration that applies

The [official Graduate visa guidance](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa) states that the usual duration is two years for qualifying applications made on or before 31 December 2026 and 18 months for applications from 1 January 2027. A PhD or other doctoral qualification gives a three-year visa.

These are application-date rules. They do not shorten every existing Graduate visa on 1 January 2027. Use the individual’s actual immigration result for the permission relevant to employment.

## Read work restrictions before assigning the role

Graduate permission generally allows work in most jobs, self-employment and looking for work. It does not allow work as a professional sportsperson. Check the actual role and the restrictions shown in the person’s result rather than treating the route name as unrestricted permission for every activity.

Use the employer-facing online right-to-work service where appropriate. Retain its result and connect it to the person and the work being offered. A visa screenshot or a university completion letter alone does not replace the prescribed process.

## Plan any later sponsorship separately

A Graduate visa cannot be extended. A person may be able to switch to another route if they meet that route’s requirements. Continuing the job under a Skilled Worker visa requires an eligible sponsor, eligible role and the salary and other requirements applicable to the particular case.

There is no single universal “Graduate sponsorship cost”. Fees, the immigration skills charge, the person’s circumstances and who pays which permitted cost need separate consideration. Do not promise that an existing employee can switch successfully without checking the rules.

## Track the next review without assuming the outcome

Keep the current check result, permission date and next required follow-up with the employment record. Begin any business discussion about future sponsorship early enough for a proper assessment, while keeping that planning discussion separate from the statutory check.

If an in-time application is pending at expiry, follow the Home Office process for that situation. Do not assume either that work must stop immediately or that the person can always continue.
