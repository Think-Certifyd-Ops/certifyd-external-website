This article is part of Certifyd’s historical security editorial archive. It was revised in October 2026 to remove unsupported claims and earlier product promises. It is not a specification of currently available Certifyd features.


## Ask what a result establishes

A biometric comparison, document review and permission-to-work result answer different questions. A claimed accuracy figure needs a defined evaluation, population and error measure. This article does not substantiate its earlier benchmark numbers or a deployed Certifyd biometric stack.

The [UK digital verification services trust-framework collection](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/uk-digital-verification-services-trust-framework) explains the framework. Certification has a defined service and scope; it is not a claim that every participating business can perform every statutory check.

## Keep employer actions explicit

Use the [Home Office employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) for eligible right-to-work routes. Not every person uses an immigration share code, and a generic identity result is not itself a statutory excuse.
