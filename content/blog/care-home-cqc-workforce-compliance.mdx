A workforce file should help a care-provider manager explain why a person is suitable for the work assigned. Its format matters less than whether the evidence and decisions are complete, accurate and accessible to the right people.

## Build the record around the role

For providers in England, [CQC Regulation 19](https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-regulation/providers/regulations-service-providers-and-managers/health-social-care-act/regulation-19) sets out fit-and-proper-person requirements. Identify the information and checks relevant to the actual role. Include qualifications, professional registration where applicable and the suitability assessment, alongside recruitment evidence.

Do not describe a commercial digital-record system as a mandatory CQC requirement. A digital workflow can improve retrieval, but the organisation must still meet the underlying obligations and control access to sensitive information.

## Record the check and its meaning

A DBS certificate has no official expiry date. Distinguish the issue date, any appropriate Update Service check and the provider's own review policy. Follow [DBS eligibility guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/dbs-check-requests-guidance-for-employers); a generic “DBS passed” label cannot explain the check level or suitability decision.

For immigration evidence, use the [right-to-work guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide). A visa date does not always settle whether a person may continue working: outstanding applications and verification notices have specific rules. A worker with EUSS settled or pre-settled status does not need repeat employment checks under the current guidance.

## Demonstrate how gaps are handled

Give each missing requirement a named owner and next action. Keep the original source, reviewer and decision together. Explain any deployment restriction and ensure the scheduler can see the relevant decision without seeing unnecessary personal data.

Do not infer an inspection rating from one missing document or promise that a completed software checklist guarantees a favourable result. The Fair Work Agency's labour-market remit is separate from CQC regulation and Home Office illegal-working enforcement.
