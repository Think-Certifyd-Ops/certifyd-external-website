Calling someone a contractor does not settle their employment, tax or immigration position. These frameworks have different tests, and a label in an invoice or worker database cannot replace the assessment.

## Establish the actual relationship

Record the parties, agreement, work and management arrangements. Review relevant employment-status guidance and obtain advice where the facts are uncertain. A contractual description should match how the work operates.

For right-to-work responsibility, use the [current Home Office guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide). Its newly covered worker-contract, individual-subcontractor and matching-service arrangements use employment commencing on or after 1 October 2026. The separate extended-liability provisions use relevant contractual arrangements entered into on or after that date. “Self-employed” is not a universal exemption, and “customer” is not a universal checker. Review the applicable categories, commencement rules and exclusions.

## Assess tax rules separately

[HMRC's off-payroll working guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/understanding-off-payroll-working-ir35) concerns services through an intermediary and the relevant tax responsibilities. It is not an immigration check. The rules distinguish public-sector, medium or large private-sector and small private-sector clients.

Do not apply a single turnover threshold as the complete small-company test. Use the current criteria and any relevant group or timing rules. A tax-status decision alone does not establish that the worker has permission for the proposed activity.

## Keep the decisions and evidence distinct

Record the status assessment, prescribed check responsibility, role requirements and site authorisation separately. Agree who receives changes to the work or supplying organisation. If the arrangement changes, review the decisions that depended on it.

Give an unresolved issue an owner and action. Avoid promising that one identity verification or profile makes all downstream contractual relationships compliant.
