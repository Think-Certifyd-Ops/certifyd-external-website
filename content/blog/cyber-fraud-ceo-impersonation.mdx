## Confirm authority through the process

A request presented as coming from a senior colleague still needs the normal approval route. Use established independent contact details when a request concerns payment, account access or a change to sensitive information.

The [NCSC phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) explains organisational controls and reporting. Do not rely entirely on staff recognising the tone, face or voice of the apparent requester.

## Label fraud data precisely

Total payment-fraud losses, authorised push-payment fraud and executive impersonation are different categories. This article does not substantiate an earlier headline total as the cost of CEO fraud.

Certifyd's current [worker-records workflow](/platform/records/) supports evidence and review actions. It does not authenticate every meeting attendee or guarantee that executive impersonation and unauthorised payments cannot occur.
