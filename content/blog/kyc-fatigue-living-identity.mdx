This article is part of Certifyd’s historical security editorial archive. It was revised in October 2026 to remove unsupported claims and earlier product promises. It is not a specification of currently available Certifyd features.


## Reuse still needs a receiving decision

A reused document can reduce repeated collection, but the receiving organisation must understand the evidence's source, date and permitted purpose. The original review may not cover a new role or changed circumstances.

For prescribed employer checks, follow the [Home Office guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide). Another organisation's identity profile does not automatically transfer its statutory excuse or replace required employer actions.

## Avoid claims of perpetual validity

“Living identity” is a concept, not proof that a system continuously verifies every attribute. An identity graph or reciprocal verification process needs a defined implementation, consent model and scope.

Certifyd currently organises [worker evidence, review dates and attendance](/platform/records/). This historical article does not promise a deployed universal identity graph, automatic compliance or unrestricted portability across every business relationship.
