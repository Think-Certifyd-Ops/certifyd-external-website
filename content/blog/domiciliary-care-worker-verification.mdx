Domiciliary care moves the handoff away from a staffed reception desk. The provider needs a reliable way to connect the planned visit, authorised worker and evidence reviewed for that role.

## Prepare the worker record before scheduling

Identify the role requirements and who reviews each one. For a regulated provider in England, consider [CQC Regulation 19](https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-regulation/providers/regulations-service-providers-and-managers/health-social-care-act/regulation-19), alongside appropriate safeguarding and professional-registration requirements. Keep the source and decision behind each requirement.

Use the [DBS guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/dbs-check-requests-guidance-for-employers) for eligibility and check level. A certificate has no official expiry date and is not a guarantee of present suitability. Keep work-permission evidence in its separate process, using [current employer guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide).

## Plan for a changed worker

The scheduler should know which person is expected and whether the placement is authorised. A substitute should trigger a revised handoff, including the role requirements and any unresolved action. Do not assume the original worker's evidence covers a replacement.

Tell the person receiving care how the provider communicates legitimate changes and whom to contact if something is unexpected. Avoid sharing passports, DBS disclosures or immigration details as a way to reassure a household.

## Record the visit without overstating its meaning

An attendance entry can show what was recorded for a visit. It is different from proof that all employment and safeguarding checks were completed or that care was delivered to the required standard. Review discrepancies against the actual schedule and contact the appropriate manager.

Give a missed handoff an owner and next action. Where there is a safeguarding concern, follow the provider's procedure and the relevant emergency or referral route; a record update is not a substitute for action.
