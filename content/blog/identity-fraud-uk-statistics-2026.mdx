## State the source and denominator

[Cifas Fraudscape 2026](https://www.fraudscape.co.uk/) reports 242,003 identity-fraud filings in the National Fraud Database during 2025. That was 3% fewer than 2024 and 54% of its recorded cases.

These are filings in a defined member database, not a count of every fraud committed in the UK. The data does not establish an AI-generated employment-document share or a particular organisation's annual financial exposure.

## Do not turn totals into product proof

A national total cannot show that an identity product prevents a specific loss. Additional claims about sectors, growth, cost and causes need corresponding evidence.

Certifyd's current [worker-records offer](/platform/records/) concerns evidence, review dates and attendance. It does not automatically query all fraud databases or guarantee that a workforce contains no fraudulent identity.
