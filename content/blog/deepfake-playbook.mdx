## Evaluate the evidence and request together

An unexpected message or call needs review of the requested action, contact route and supporting evidence. Visual or audio clues may prompt a concern, but absence of a clue is not proof that the interaction is genuine.

The [NCSC phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) recommends layered controls and a response plan. It does not reduce security to a single detection technique.

## Avoid categorical product claims

Cryptographic and identity systems have a defined scope, implementation and threat model. This article does not claim that all visual advice is obsolete or that any commercial system makes impersonation mathematically impossible.
