For schools and colleges in England, safer recruitment starts with the role and relationship. Staff, supply workers, volunteers and visitors do not all have identical checking or record requirements.

## Use the current education guidance

[Keeping children safe in education](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/keeping-children-safe-in-education--2) was updated for September 2026. Its scope is schools and colleges in England; do not apply every provision as a universal rule for universities or education organisations throughout the UK.

Review the requirements for the particular role, including agency or supply arrangements and relevant overseas information. Record the decision and evidence the organisation needs rather than requiring an identical package from every visitor.

## Keep the single central record accurate

The guidance sets out who must appear on the single central record and what it records. It is not a register of every volunteer and visitor. Check the relevant categories and required entries, and keep supporting sensitive evidence appropriately restricted.

For DBS checks, assess eligibility and barred-list requirements under the current rules. The [removal of the supervision exemption](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/working-or-volunteering-in-regulated-activity-with-children/regulated-activity-removal-of-the-supervision-exemption-comes-into-force-1-september-2026) came into force on 1 September 2026. Do not reuse an older checklist that assumes the former exemption still applies.

## Separate permission and suitability

A DBS certificate has no official expiry date and is not an immigration check. Use the [right-to-work guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) for the applicable employer process. For supplied staff, clarify the supplying organisation's checks and the receiving school's required confirmation and identity actions.

If an entry or assurance is missing, record the issue and the authorised decision about deployment. A complete software checklist does not guarantee an inspection result or replace a safeguarding assessment.
