Right-to-work evidence contains personal information. Keep what the prescribed process requires, explain its purpose and give access to the people who need it for that purpose.

## Define what the check requires

The [Home Office employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) sets out retained evidence for each method. Right-to-work evidence is normally retained for the duration of employment and two years afterwards. Follow the applicable requirements rather than keeping every document a candidate has ever supplied.

A physical biometric residence permit is not a substitute for an online right-to-work result. Retain the employer result and relevant check evidence, rather than relying on an old card copy as proof of current permission.

## Explain lawful processing and access

Document the purpose and applicable lawful basis in your organisation's privacy information. Use separate access rules for managers making placement decisions and staff who must inspect sensitive source documents. Avoid exposing passports or immigration history in a broadly shared attendance view.

Record corrections without silently rewriting the history of a check. If the evidence is disputed, preserve an appropriate audit record and follow the organisation's review process. A date in software should accurately describe the action taken.

## Apply a reasoned retention schedule

The [ICO storage-limitation guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/) requires personal information to be kept only as long as needed for its purposes. The prescribed right-to-work period does not automatically define the retention of payroll, litigation, criminal-record or unrelated HR information. Assess those separately.

Include backups, exports and access removal in the schedule. Do not promise instant destruction from every backup unless the actual technical process supports that claim. Document any justified hold and review it when the reason ends.
