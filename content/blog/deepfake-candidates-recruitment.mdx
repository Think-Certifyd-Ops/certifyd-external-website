## Review identity and job evidence separately

The [FBI's June 2022 advisory](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2022/PSA220628) describes complaints involving deepfakes and stolen information in remote-work applications. It is evidence of reported patterns, not a percentage of all candidates or a universal voice-detection benchmark.

Maintain the employer's controlled recruitment process and relevant role checks. A convincing interview does not establish qualifications or work permission. A suspicious presentation also needs a fair review before an allegation is made.

## Keep statutory checks distinct

Use the [current employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) for the eligible right-to-work process. A QR profile does not automatically establish identity, permission or a statutory excuse in a remote interview.
