## Use the official account

A [Hong Kong government statement from June 2024](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202406/26/P2024062600192p.htm) describes a fraud involving a prerecorded video conference and transfers totalling about HK$200 million. It reports police believed the material used downloaded public footage and voices; this was their assessment during an ongoing investigation.

That account does not establish the detailed real-time technical architecture previously described in this article. Avoid treating assumptions about the call as observed facts or converting the amount into an undated exchange-rate claim.

## Keep payment authorisation independent

Organisations should review exceptional payment requests through controlled approval and independent contact routes. A convincing conference appearance does not replace those controls.

This incident is not evidence that a particular Certifyd product would have prevented the loss. The current [workforce-records scope](/platform/records/) is separate from meeting authentication and payment-fraud prevention.
