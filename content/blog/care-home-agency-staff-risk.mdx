When an agency worker arrives for a shift, the care home needs to know who is expected, what role they will perform and which placement requirements have been addressed. An agency booking confirmation should lead to a clear handoff.

## Agree the evidence handoff before the shift

Set out which records the agency supplies and which decisions the care provider makes. Include the named worker, role, relevant qualifications or registration, appropriate safeguarding evidence and restrictions affecting the placement.

[CQC Regulation 19](https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-regulation/providers/regulations-service-providers-and-managers/health-social-care-act/regulation-19) concerns fit and proper persons employed by providers in England. Review the requirements applicable to the role and arrangement. Do not interpret an agency agreement as proof that the provider has no suitability responsibilities.

## Keep separate checks separate

Use [DBS guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/dbs-check-requests-guidance-for-employers) to assess eligibility and the relevant level of check. Certificates have no official expiry date. A local renewal policy should be labelled as a review policy, with a decision about the evidence needed for this role.

Right-to-work responsibility depends on the actual arrangement and [current guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide). A hirer does not automatically have to repeat an agency's prescribed check in every placement. Obtain the appropriate confirmation and evidence for the relationship instead of presenting duplicated uploads as a legal rule.

## Reconcile the arrival with the booking

Give the receiving manager the expected worker and placement decision. If a substitute arrives, check the revised authorisation before allocating duties. A shared profile or QR attendance record can support this handoff; it does not itself establish DBS suitability, registration or a statutory excuse.

Record discrepancies, who reviewed them and the outcome. Keep unresolved issues visible to the manager responsible for the shift. Avoid putting sensitive criminal-record detail into a broadly shared rota.
