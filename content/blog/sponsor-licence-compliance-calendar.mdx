A sponsorship calendar needs more than recurring reminders. It needs a way to catch changes as they happen, because a quarterly review can come after an event's reporting deadline.

## Create event-specific reporting entries

Use [Part 3 of the sponsor guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-3-sponsor-duties-and-compliance/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-3-sponsor-duties-and-compliance-accessible) to identify reportable worker and organisation changes. Record the event date, applicable deadline and authorised owner. Do not apply a universal ten-working-day rule: the guidance distinguishes different types of report and their time limits.

The usual reporting deadline is ten working days for worker changes and twenty working days for organisational changes, subject to exceptions. For unauthorised absence exceeding ten consecutive working days, report no later than ten working days after the tenth absence day. Use the particular rule when calculating the action's due date.

Connect the sponsorship owner to payroll, line managers and staff who manage work locations. Define how they raise a change and what information the report needs. A reminder cannot compensate for an event that nobody tells the owner about.

## Keep planned reviews separate

Schedule internal reviews of access, contact details, recruitment records and the documents listed in [Appendix D](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/keep-records-for-sponsorship-appendix-d). These reviews help find gaps; they do not extend a legal reporting deadline or create a statutory excuse.

Use meaningful review dates for time-limited permission and verification notices. The date printed on a document may not be the relevant follow-up date. EUSS settled and pre-settled status do not require repeat employment checks under the current right-to-work guidance. See the [follow-up guide](/blog/right-to-work-follow-up-checks/) before setting a blanket annual immigration check.

## Close an action with evidence

For each completed action, retain the actual report or check result and its completion date. Record an exception with an owner and next step. A green calendar icon should mean the specified action was completed, not that somebody dismissed a notification.

Review workload and cover before holidays or staff departures. Keep unresolved matters visible until a responsible person has made and recorded the decision.
