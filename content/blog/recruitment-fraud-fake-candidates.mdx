## Review claims against their source

The [FBI's June 2022 advisory](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2022/PSA220628) describes reported misuse of deepfakes and stolen information in remote-work applications. It does not establish the earlier candidate-count or CV-fraud percentages in this article.

Use the employer's recruitment and role-checking process. Give discrepancies a fair review rather than turning an uncertain document or unusual interview into an automatic accusation.

## Keep the check's meaning clear

Work permission, qualifications, identity evidence and performance are different questions. Use the [employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) for prescribed right-to-work checks. A profile does not continuously authenticate a candidate through every later interaction.

Certifyd's current [records workflow](/platform/records/) supports evidence and review decisions. It does not guarantee fraud-free applicants or a universal continuous identity chain.
