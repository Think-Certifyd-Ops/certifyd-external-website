Certifyd helps workforce teams connect the worker, their evidence and the next review action. The aim is a record a manager can use when preparing work, resolving a gap or reconciling attendance.

## Start with the worker record

The [records view](/platform/records/) brings role requirements, source evidence and expiry or review dates into the same workflow. A received document, completed review and attendance event remain different things. The team needs to know what was reviewed and what still requires a decision.

Use the [worker-record review checklist](/resources/worker-record-review-checklist/) to identify owners and missing actions. For a supplied worker, clarify the agency's checks and the receiving organisation's placement evidence before assuming everyone must repeat the same process.

## Keep legal checks with the responsible organisation

Prescribed right-to-work checks follow the [Home Office employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide). Safeguarding, professional registration and sponsor reporting have their own requirements. A software profile or QR attendance entry does not automatically establish those checks or guarantee a statutory excuse.

The current offer does not claim universal biometric authentication, access to every issuing-authority database or immunity from identity fraud. Historical security concepts on this site should not be read as currently available workforce features.

Bring a role, typical evidence requirement and an example of the handoff your team needs to improve. The [demo booking page](/contact/) lets you select the topics you want to discuss, including tracked records, workforce overview, reusable worker documents and site check-in.
