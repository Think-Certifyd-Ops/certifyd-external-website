## Review the framework and service scope

The [UK digital verification services trust-framework collection](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/uk-digital-verification-services-trust-framework) sets out the government's digital identity framework. A framework does not mean every identity record is universally portable or accepted for every statutory purpose.

Check the service, certification scope and receiving organisation's requirements. A planned standard or interoperability goal is different from a capability deployed across all organisations.

## Preserve the decision boundary

For employer checks, use the [Home Office guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide). Reusing a document or profile does not automatically transfer another organisation's statutory excuse or remove required employer actions.

Certifyd's current [worker-records scope](/platform/records/) includes evidence and review workflows. This archive does not promise a universal UK identity infrastructure, government integration or automatic acceptance of every reusable credential.
