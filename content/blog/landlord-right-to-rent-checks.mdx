Right to rent concerns access to residential accommodation in England. It is separate from an employer's right-to-work check and should have its own process and evidence record.

## Establish whether the tenancy is covered

Use the [current landlord guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/landlords-guide-to-right-to-rent-checks/landlords-guide-to-right-to-rent-checks-1-october-2026-accessible) to identify the accommodation, adults and responsible party covered by the scheme. Do not apply the England framework as though it were a UK-wide housing requirement.

Where an agent performs checks, clarify the responsibility in the agreement. Keep the evidence required by the applicable process, rather than assuming an employment check or letting-platform identity badge is sufficient.

## Choose the correct route and timing

The guidance explains manual checks, the Home Office online service, eligible digital verification and the Landlord Checking Service. Physical biometric residence permits are not a substitute for the required online result.

For a person with a time-limited right to rent, conduct the initial check no earlier than 28 days before the tenancy agreement is entered into, usually when it is signed. This can differ from the date they move in. Do not apply that timing rule indiscriminately to every person and evidence type. British and Irish citizens have their own acceptable routes.

## Set a follow-up from the applicable rules

A follow-up is not always due on the visa date shown in an old copy. Use the guide's rules for the evidence and statutory excuse in the particular case. Pending applications and Home Office verification results require their own review.

Record the check date, retained result, relevant follow-up and owner. Apply the policy fairly and avoid selecting tenants for extra checks because of accent, appearance or assumed nationality.

This article explains the separate process; it is not a claim that Certifyd performs statutory right-to-rent verification. For employer records, see [right-to-work documents](/blog/right-to-work-check-documents-list/). If an arrangement or immigration position is uncertain, obtain advice before taking action against a tenant.
