## A plausible participant is not an approval

Before acting on an unusual request in a meeting, use the organisation's established decision and independent-contact process. A familiar face or voice does not itself authorise a transfer, secret disclosure or account change.

The [Hong Kong official account](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202406/26/P2024062600192p.htm) illustrates a fraud involving a prerecorded conference. It is not evidence for the separate unsourced law-firm loss previously described in this article.

## Check the service actually offered

Meeting authentication requires a defined system and evaluation. This archive does not promise an unbeatable identity method or a currently available Certifyd Verify meeting service.

Certifyd's current [workforce-records workflow](/platform/records/) supports evidence and review actions. It is separate from authenticating every remote meeting participant.
