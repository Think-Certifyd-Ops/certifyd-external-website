Preventing illegal working requires an assessment of permission and the applicable responsibilities. A recruitment assurance or stored document is useful evidence only when it supports the required process.

## Identify the responsible arrangement

Use the [current employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) to review the contract and actual work. It addresses direct employment and specified other arrangements. An agency's involvement does not automatically remove all responsibility, but it also does not mean every hirer must repeat every agency check.

Keep right to rent separate. That is a different framework for residential accommodation in England; see our [landlord guide](/blog/landlord-right-to-rent-checks/). A housing check does not replace a work check.

## Complete and retain the eligible check

Use the appropriate manual, online, eligible digital-provider or Employer Checking Service route. Inspect the result against the person and actual role. Keep the prescribed evidence and check date, together with any follow-up requirement.

An emailed scan, bank account or National Insurance number alone is not proof of a completed right-to-work check. Do not describe an identity profile or attendance QR code as a statutory excuse.

## Understand the penalty framework

The [civil penalty code](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/illegal-working-penalties-codes-of-practice-for-employers) concerns illegal working and the employer's position under the scheme. A missing check can leave an employer without an excuse, but a missing file alone is not a finding that someone worked illegally or that a penalty is payable.

Knowledge of illegal working and reasonably apparent document falsity also matter. A software supplier cannot promise immunity. If an official notice arrives, act on its grounds and deadlines and get advice on the case.

Where permission appears time-sensitive, review pending-application and verification-notice rules before making an employment decision. Do not apply one universal curtailment period to all sponsor problems or workers.
