This article is part of Certifyd’s historical security editorial archive. It was revised in October 2026 to remove unsupported claims and earlier product promises. It is not a specification of currently available Certifyd features.


## Separate a forecast from an observed result

Predictions about AI-enabled attacks need a stated method, period and uncertainty. This article does not substantiate the earlier vulnerability-count forecast or percentage claims. A dramatic phrase is not evidence of what an organisation will experience.

For operational planning, identify the systems, permissions and data an automated tool can access. Review changes through the organisation's security process and maintain a route for reporting unexpected behaviour.

## Use established controls

The [NCSC's guidance on phishing](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) illustrates a layered approach to prevention and response. It does not suggest that a single identity product makes attacks impossible.

Certifyd's earlier universal-security concepts are not current feature specifications. The available [workforce-records workflow](/platform/records/) concerns evidence, review actions and attendance, rather than autonomous-agent threat prevention or a forecast of future cyber incidents.
