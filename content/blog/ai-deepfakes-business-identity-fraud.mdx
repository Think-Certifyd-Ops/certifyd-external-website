## Review the action, not only the appearance

The [FBI's June 2022 advisory](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2022/PSA220628) describes complaints involving deepfakes and stolen personal information in applications for remote work. Its date and scope matter: it is not a 2024 census of all recruitment fraud.

For an unexpected request, use the organisation's established verification and approval process. A plausible face or voice does not independently authorise a payment, disclose a secret or change a worker's role.

## Avoid absolute detection or prevention claims

Capability depends on the attack, evidence and system. This article does not substantiate a universal production cost, a three-second cloning threshold or mathematical immunity from impersonation.

Certifyd's current [workforce records](/platform/records/) help organise evidence and human review. They do not provide a deepfake detector or a universal authentication layer for all business communication.
