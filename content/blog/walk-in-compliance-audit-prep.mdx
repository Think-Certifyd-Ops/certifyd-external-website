Preparation begins with knowing which records exist, who owns them and what each result means. A promise to retrieve everything in minutes is less useful than an honest review of the actual evidence gaps.

## Identify the framework and request

Read any official request and establish the organisation and matters concerned. Home Office right-to-work enforcement, sponsor compliance, the Fair Work Agency and sector regulation have different remits. Do not describe the Fair Work Agency as the source of illegal-working civil penalties.

For work permission, use the [employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide). Sponsors also need [Part 3](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-3-sponsor-duties-and-compliance/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-3-sponsor-duties-and-compliance-accessible) and [Appendix D](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/keep-records-for-sponsorship-appendix-d). Regulated services may have additional workforce requirements.

## Reconcile a sample of worker records

Compare the worker list with the actual roles and assignments. Locate the prescribed check result, date, reviewer and relevant restrictions. Keep a document-receipt date separate from a completed check date.

Review supplied-worker arrangements and the appropriate evidence handoff. Do not infer that every third-party worker creates automatic liability for both supplier and customer. A missing record also does not, by itself, establish illegal working.

## Make the review queue explicit

For each gap, record what is missing, the owner, next action and relevant due date. Preserve the history of a correction instead of making the file appear complete from an earlier date. Escalate uncertain permission or a disputed decision appropriately.

Give authorised staff a way to locate evidence while restricting unnecessary access to sensitive documents. Keep a record of material supplied in response to an official request and use its actual deadlines.
