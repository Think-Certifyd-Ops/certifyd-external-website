## Define which interaction is covered

A service can operate across interfaces while still having limits on the evidence and decisions it supports. An identity result for one channel does not automatically authorise a payment or validate every later interaction.

The [NCSC's layered phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) is a useful reminder to keep independent approval and response processes. “Platform agnostic” is not a security guarantee.

## Separate ambition from availability

This article does not substantiate its earlier loss narrative or a claim that one approach is the only effective form of verification. Evaluate a supplier using current documentation, actual scope and evidence.

Certifyd currently supports [workforce records and reviews](/platform/records/). Earlier universal cross-channel authentication descriptions are historical concepts, not current product commitments.
