This article is part of Certifyd’s historical security editorial archive. It was revised in October 2026 to remove unsupported claims and earlier product promises. It is not a specification of currently available Certifyd features.


## Review the request through an established route

Unexpected payment, access or information requests should follow the organisation's approval process. Use independent contact details when confirming a change rather than relying only on the message's link or phone number.

The [NCSC phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) combines technical controls, staff reporting and response. It recognises that some attacks will get through; no training or single identity feature detects them all.

## Keep loss claims sourced

This article does not substantiate its earlier case loss or represent an all-payment-fraud total as phishing losses. A claim about a category, period and amount needs the corresponding published evidence.
