## Review the action and approval route

Pressure and apparent authority can make a request difficult to question. Give staff an established route to confirm payments, account changes and sensitive disclosures, plus a way to report concerns.

The [NCSC phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) combines technical controls, reporting and response. A broad survey's “human element” category should not be relabelled as the percentage of all breaches caused by social engineering.

## Avoid false certainty

This article does not substantiate its earlier percentages or unnamed loss cases. A cryptographic system has a defined scope and implementation; it does not make every social-engineering attack impossible.

Certifyd's current [workforce records](/platform/records/) support evidence and review actions. They do not authenticate every request or remove the need for controlled approvals and incident response.
