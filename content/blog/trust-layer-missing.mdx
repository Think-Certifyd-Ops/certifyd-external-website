This article is part of Certifyd’s historical security editorial archive. It was revised in October 2026 to remove unsupported claims and earlier product promises. It is not a specification of currently available Certifyd features.


## Evaluate the proposed decision

A trust service needs to define whose evidence it uses, what it establishes and which organisation decides the next action. Infrastructure language does not remove those responsibilities.

For employment, the [Home Office guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) defines prescribed checks. A reusable identity profile is not an automatic statutory excuse or universal permission for every role.

## Keep product ambitions separate

This article does not claim that Certifyd is deployed across every interaction, automatically completes every compliance process or achieves unmeasured customer savings. Those assertions need actual implementation and outcome evidence.

Certifyd's current [workforce-records scope](/platform/records/) concerns evidence, role requirements, review dates and attendance. The earlier universal trust-layer proposal is historical context rather than an available-service specification.
