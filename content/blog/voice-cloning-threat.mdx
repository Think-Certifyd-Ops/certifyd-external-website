This article is part of Certifyd’s historical security editorial archive. It was revised in October 2026 to remove unsupported claims and earlier product promises. It is not a specification of currently available Certifyd features.


## A familiar voice is one signal

An unexpected request should use the organisation's established authorisation and independent-contact process. The sound of a caller alone should not authorise payment, account access or disclosure of sensitive information.

The [NCSC phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) recommends layered controls and reporting. Staff should be able to question a request without relying on their ability to detect every manipulation.

## Keep capability claims bounded

This article does not substantiate its earlier accuracy percentage or fixed audio-duration threshold. Voice synthesis performance varies with the material, technique and evaluation conditions.

Certifyd's current [workforce records](/platform/records/) concern evidence and review actions. This archive does not promise a voice-cloning detector or universal authentication of phone calls.
