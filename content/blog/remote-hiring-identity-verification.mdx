## Choose the eligible employer process

The [Home Office guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checks-employers-guide) distinguishes manual, online, eligible digital-provider and Employer Checking Service routes. Remote hiring does not turn an emailed scan into a completed prescribed check.

A live video identity comparison can support the manual route where the employer physically holds the required originals. This differs from reviewing only images supplied during a call. Complete the route's required actions and retain the employer evidence.

## Keep recruitment questions distinct

A right-to-work result does not establish qualifications, performance or every future communication's authenticity. Review those matters through their appropriate processes.
