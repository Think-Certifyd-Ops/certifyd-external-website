## A practical question for the listener

How does your team verify an unexpected request involving payment, account access or sensitive information? Staff need an established approval route and a way to report concerns without relying only on whether a caller or message seems familiar.

The [NCSC phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) describes layered technical and organisational controls. A culture of asking questions supports those controls; it does not replace them.
