## Review the requested action

An unexpected message or call needs a controlled approval route when it concerns payment, account access or sensitive information. Familiar presentation is not the only evidence to consider. The [NCSC phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/phishing) recommends layered controls, reporting and incident response.
