Keep useful guides current and connected.

Four priority articles retain their existing URLs and publication dates, with a genuine updated date: employer document list, follow-up checks, agency responsibilities and care records. The two new checklists provide a concrete next action. The two existing resource guides and their reading pages now use the simpler design and current source links.

Keep the articles useful without requiring a booking. Add platform/records links only where they help the reader do the next task. Distinguish records storage from prescribed checks, review dates from expiry dates, and a scan from continuous presence.

The wider 88-article archive still needs a separate factual/claim refresh. Prioritise statutory-excuse, discrimination, Graduate visa and old Fair Work Agency articles using fresh official sources. Retaining the archive is not evidence that every historic legal or product claim has been revalidated. No mass expansion or blanket redirects are proposed for that archive.

The five AI-fraud product families, variants, support/security pages and associated deepfake assessment are removed from the exported website and force-redirected to the homepage, as requested. Earlier platform/sector consolidation routes keep their intent-matched destinations. Both slash forms are covered.

Check the exact redirect map. Retired routes stay out of navigation and the sitemap. Preserve necessary legal/account routes until the separate brand has verified replacements. Source changes are on the review branch; live redirects must be checked after deployment.

Measure the journey through a real booking.

The review branch records consent-approved demo CTA clicks separately from accepted Cal booking completion. The handler validates the Cal origin, iframe source, namespace and success status and sends no booking name, email, UID or meeting time to GA. Pending, incomplete and rescheduled events do not count as new accepted bookings.

The live GA key-event setting remains unconfirmed: Google OAuth could not resolve from this environment, so no setting changed. Use the configuration evidence to complete it once access is restored. Then inspect the actual collector and booking result on the deployed build. Do not use clicks as evidence of bookings.

The static export, TypeScript check, lint, workload arithmetic and measurement-boundary checks pass. The metadata audit checks all exported index routes for one H1 and their canonical URLs; the internal-link audit resolves exported links and configured rewrites. Testimonials and review-rating schema have been removed from current marketing source.

Browser-rendered desktop/mobile fit, keyboard interactions, real demo assets and the live booking/redirect journey remain release checks. Browser access is currently denied. The local review bundle is for content/design review, with initial designs for React-only tools and no submitted enquiries.

Compare the right signals after release.

Record the release date and retain the current baseline. Review crawling and indexation first, then compare 28-day windows by page group: platform, outcomes, sectors and employer guides. Track impressions, clicks and click-through rate alongside consented CTA clicks, accepted bookings and qualified employer conversations.

Review the document guide’s title and description against the queries it actually earns. Judge sector pages on enough impressions to make a comparison. Add new pages when they answer a distinct buyer task with product evidence, rather than using article volume as a target.
