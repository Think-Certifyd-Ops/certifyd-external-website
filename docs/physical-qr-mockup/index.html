Inside a care home

Track check-ins to each care-home room.

Place a display by the room or care point. Each scan adds the worker, room and time to the visit record.

Review room check-ins alongside the wider shift history. Keep resident details out of the QR code.

In domiciliary care

Record home visits, even without a connection.

Leave a small display at the agreed point in the home. Care workers scan on arrival and at the other points your visit process requires.

Keep recording when the connection drops. The app saves the scan and syncs it when the worker reconnects.

A dedicated device

Replace a check-in tablet with a compact display.

Put a lower-cost device at each check-in point. The display shows the changing code; workers use their own phones to scan it.

Fit it beside an entrance, a room door or a home-visit point. Its small enclosure and low power use help you place more scan points where your team needs them.

Keep each check-in point small.

Mount the display. Assign the location. Let your team scan with the app.
