CERTIFYD HEADING AND PAGE-HIERARCHY REVIEW
12 October 2026 | Source: current local export of design/internal-pages-2026-10-12

Scope: 167 exported HTML pages inventoried; 34 commercial/editorial review pages examined heading by heading. /platform/ is retired and redirects to /. /platform/records/ remains. The physical rotating-QR page is a standalone mockup, not a live route.

DECISION
Keep the homepage as the platform overview. Explain five buyer tasks through the outcome pages, then use sector pages for the situations those buyers recognise. An active verb alone is insufficient: each major heading should name the actual task or object. Use plain descriptive titles for legal pages and editorial articles where an imperative would be artificial.

CHANGES ALREADY MADE IN THIS PASS
The homepage chapter callouts and fake UI instruction lines were removed. CTA and solution-row arrows now point right and move right on hover. The hero now uses the saved Certifyd role/workspace capture. /platform/ was removed from navigation, sitemap and the review bundle, with a redirect to /. The five outcome pages and tracked-records page received action-led H1/H2 revisions. The missing-page heading now says “We couldn’t find this page.”

IMPORTANT VISUAL LIMIT
The saved image public/images/connected/roles.png is an existing product capture, not a fresh capture from the live app. It should be replaced with a current, sanitised workforce/records view once access is available. Check its seeded numbers, redactions and permissions before publishing. Browser access to the live app was denied during this pass, so the updated image and hover motion need human visual review.
