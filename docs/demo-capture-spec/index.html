Capture the evidence behind the Certifyd story.

Page-by-page platform demo specification · 12 October 2026

Use real product screens to replace the current fictional examples. This specification records what to capture and where to put it.

Exact slot IDs and capture requirements

Prepare one clean demo dataset.

  • Use a seeded demo organisation and fictional workers; remove real names, emails, documents, IDs, addresses and tokens.
  • Capture real supported behaviour and version/date each asset. Do not simulate absent features as product proof.
  • Record desktop at 1440×900 and mobile at 390×844; export 16:10 desktop posters and native phone aspect ratios. Crop deliberately; never stretch.
  • Use muted captioned video with a poster, explicit play/pause, reduced-motion support and a static fallback. No autoplay audio.
  • Keep hero previews compact. Place longer clips in the matching chapter; use the existing data-demo-slot attributes to locate them.
  • Document which states and permissions each capture establishes. Use genuine client evidence only after permission; Certifyd has no clients/testimonials to use now.

Connected worker record overview

Format: 15–25 second captioned company-workspace clip, still poster at 16:10

  1. Open a fictional worker from the workforce view
  2. Show role requirements, shared personal evidence and company-held records
  3. Follow one item to its evidence and next review action

Claim boundary: A connected view does not imply the organisation owns all personal documents or that every status is independently verified.

Role requirements and onboarding

Format: 20-second desktop capture; 16:10 poster

  1. Choose a role in the company workspace
  2. Show the evidence requested for that role
  3. Show an invitation and a worker who has not yet supplied all evidence

Claim boundary: Use the actual supported onboarding flow. Do not replace unimplemented invitations/import with animation presented as product.

Import and first-group review

Format: 30-second desktop walkthrough with a sanitised sample spreadsheet and a downloadable example template

  1. Show the accepted import or assisted-import method
  2. Map person, role and record fields through the real supported process
  3. Demonstrate duplicate/missing-field handling and first-group review before wider rollout

Claim boundary: Confirm self-serve versus assisted import. Do not imply automatic mapping or validation without a working screen and repeatable result.

Workforce overview and evidence drill-down

Format: 20–30 second desktop clip; 16:10 overview and detail posters

  1. Filter missing evidence and pending reviews
  2. Open one worker record from the overview
  3. Show the document, its source, status and reviewer’s next action

Claim boundary: An upload is not a completed review. Avoid fabricated workforce totals or claimed reduction in compliance risk.

Expiry, review dates and history

Format: 20-second desktop clip covering due and renewed states

  1. Show a real expiry field and a separate organisation review date
  2. Open an approaching-date record
  3. Add replacement evidence through the real workflow and inspect history

Claim boundary: A credential expiry date is not automatically a statutory follow-up date. DBS certificates have no official expiry date.

Personal and organisation ownership

Format: Paired desktop and mobile captures, shown sequentially rather than a three-column montage

  1. Worker opens personal credentials in the app
  2. Worker shares the relevant record for work
  3. Organisation sees only shared evidence plus its own documents and review decision

Claim boundary: Verify permissions and what survives leaving an organisation. Do not suggest unrestricted access to the worker’s wallet.

Worker app and scoped document sharing

Format: 390×844 mobile clip plus 16:10 company-review capture

  1. Open a fictional personal ID document with masked identifying details
  2. Share the relevant document through the actual permission flow
  3. Show company access and the pending review; demonstrate removal/expiry only if actually supported

Claim boundary: Reuse of document evidence does not complete an employer’s required identity or right-to-work check. Do not display real passports, addresses or document numbers.

App scan, attendance and visit history

Format: 15-second mobile arrival clip plus desktop attendance/history clip

  1. Worker scans the assigned site code using the actual app
  2. Manager sees the recorded person/site/time
  3. Worker checks out and manager opens the retained visit record

Claim boundary: A scan records an event. It does not alone prove uninterrupted presence, authorised work or prevent a live relay.

Homeowner shared profile

Format: Worker-phone QR presentation followed by a homeowner browser capture

  1. Tradesperson presents the real shared-profile QR
  2. Homeowner scans without accessing private documents
  3. Show person, company affiliation, role and credentials actually shared; include expired/unavailable/access-denied state

Claim boundary: Confirm no-app versus app-required homeowner flow. Show the source/status of company affiliations and credentials; do not imply independent verification beyond actual checks.

Hardware and offline check-in

Format: Real hardware macro shot, 10-second rotation clip, app scan, offline-to-synced event clip

  1. Show actual enclosure and mounting scale beside a room door or check-in point
  2. Record the display changing its authenticated code
  3. Show a valid scan and rejected stale-code example under the implemented protocol
  4. Turn off network in a sandbox, record a visit, reconnect and show the same event syncing once

Claim boundary: Validate device price, display type, rotation interval, clock drift/rollback, token signing/expiry, power draw and battery runtime. Demonstrate offline verification/time trust and replay/relay limits. No inferred “full visit presence”.

Access and data handling

Format: Desktop permission walkthrough and plain-language data diagram

  1. Invite a reviewer with limited role access
  2. Compare permitted and denied evidence views
  3. Remove access and inspect an audit entry where supported

Claim boundary: Use implemented controls. Confirm encryption, storage region, retention, processors and deletion with actual system evidence before adding certifications or security badges.
